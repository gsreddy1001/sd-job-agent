1. About the app

SD Job Agent ("the app") is a personal automation tool built and operated by Sudhakara Reddy Gorla. It searches for Project Manager job postings, scores how well each one fits the operator's resume, prepares tailored resume documents in the operator's Google Drive, and logs them in a tracking spreadsheet.

The app is for the operator's personal, non-commercial use only. It is not offered as a product or service to anyone else, and no one else is granted access.

3. What the app does not do

  • It does not invent experience, employers, tools, certifications, or metrics. Tailoring only reorders, re-emphasizes, and rewords the operator's existing resume.
